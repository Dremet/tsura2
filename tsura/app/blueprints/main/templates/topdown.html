Introduction into “Topdown Heat” – the Matchmaking Server

Jump into fast-paced Topdown Heats with a fixed isometric camera and intense ~3-minute races whenever you want. A new Heat is automatically created as soon as you join a server, so you can jump straight into the action. Each Heat consists of 3 races, with tracks and vehicles dynamically selected from the available pools. Before every race, drivers compete in a collision-free, ghosted Qualifying session to determine the starting grid. Bots are always active to keep races populated. Once 5 human players join the server, the bots are automatically disabled, letting the Heat continue with a fully human grid.
